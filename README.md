## About

Sacred Tree is a participatory XR installation that reanimates disregarded fallen flowers as hybrid digital beings called Gelora through embodied ritual gestures and augmented media.

Rooted in Korean sacred tree traditions (신목, shinmok) and communal ritual spaces (서낭당, seonangdang), the work invites participants to reach down and tap NFC-activated portals positioned near the ground, echoing the act of picking up what has been disregarded. Each tap summons a Gelora into augmented space through the participant's mobile camera.

From pixel to voxel, every embodied gesture becomes co-authorship. Every summoning, a cosmotechnical ritual ecology where hybrid beings find relational ground through mediated touch.

## Concept

### Sacred Tree / 신목

In Korean folk tradition, a sacred tree is a living shrine where a village gathers to connect with unseen forces. Cloth, offerings, and gestures mediate the relationship between the visible and the invisible. Sacred Tree rebuilds that threshold through augmented media.

### Five-Directional Color Cosmology / 오방색

Five colored portals extend from the central tree, each mapped to a cardinal direction, season, and element from 오방색 (obangsaek), the East Asian cosmological color system.

| Direction | Korean | Element | Gelora Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| South · 남 | 火 | Fire | Blazing with the intensity of summer noon, embodying transformation and radiant power |
| Center · 중 | 土 | Earth | Dwelling at the sacred axis where all directions meet, holding the grounding force of stillness |
| North · 북 | 水 | Water | Emerging from winter's midnight depths, carrying the stillness of potential and reflection |
| East · 동 | 木 | Wood | Born from the first light of dawn, carrying the energy of spring and new beginnings |
| West · 서 | 金 | Metal | Gleaming with autumn's fading light, carrying the wisdom of release and harvest |

### Origin of Gelora

Displaced from my motherland, I found a flower on the ground. Detached. Fallen. I projected myself onto it. I did not want to be rotten.

I scanned them with silica gel, then reanimated them as hybrid beings. Gel and flora. **Gelora.**

I wanted them to be among others. So I brought them through the lens everyone carries: the mobile camera.

## Generative Soundscape

The auditory landscape of Sacred Tree is a generative soundscape driven by additive synthesis. Pentatonic harmonies tied to Eastern cosmology layer and morph in real-time, breathing in synchronization with participant interaction and augmented creatures, Gelora. Non-repeating meditative harmonies create an ever-evolving environment where sound becomes a spatial extension of the ritual.

**Sound Tech Design:** Patrick Hartono (Tone.js)
